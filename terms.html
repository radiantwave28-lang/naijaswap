📋 Standard Nigerian Export Payment Terms

  • 70–80% paid against B/L Copy — The majority of payment is released only when the seller provides a copy of the Bill of Lading (proof of shipment). No large advance payment is required.
  • 20–30% final payment on arrival & inspection — The balance is held in escrow and released only after goods arrive at the destination port and the buyer confirms quality and quantity through inspection.
  • These terms are stated in the Sales Contract from day one and apply to all B2B export transactions on NijaSwap.
Why this matters: This is the industry-standard payment structure for Nigerian commodity exports. It protects both buyer and seller — the seller ships with confidence knowing payment is secured against B/L, while the buyer retains a holdback to ensure goods match specifications on arrival. NijaSwap escrow enforces these terms automatically.

7. B2B Export Sales Contract

For all international B2B export transactions, a Sales Contract is generated automatically by NijaSwap and must be signed (digitally or physically) by both parties before shipment. The contract includes:

  • Shipping line, port of loading, and estimated port of discharge
  • Force majeure and dispute resolution clauses
