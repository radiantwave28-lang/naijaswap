Government & Institutional Partners

Nigerian Export Promotion Council (NEPC)

Official exporter verification, trade enquiries, and buyer-seller matchmaking. Provides the RC number and NEPC certificate validation used in NaijaSwap's verification process.

Institutional

Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON)

Product quality certification and standards compliance for export goods. Mandatory for certain product categories entering EU markets.

Certification

Nigeria Customs Service (NCS)

Export clearance, documentation verification, and customs bond facilitation for B2B shipments.

Institutional

Nigerian Ports Authority (NPA)

Port operations coordination, vessel scheduling, and cargo handling at Apapa, Tincan, Onne, and Calabar ports.

Institutional

EU-Nigeria Agribusiness Platform (EUNAP)

Connects Nigerian agricultural businesses with verified European buyers, investors, and facilitators. Key partnership for agri-export division.

Network

Enterprise Europe Network

European Commission network actively seeking African agricultural partnerships. French and Belgian companies looking for cashew, cocoa, sesame, shea, and soybean suppliers.

Logistics & Shipping Partners

RoRo and container freight from West Africa to European ports. Existing relationship for charcoal and bulk commodity shipments. Primary partner for European routes.

Logistics

Global container shipping with weekly sailings from Lagos (Apapa/Tincan) to Rotterdam, Hamburg, and Felixstowe. Full container load (FCL) and less than container load (LCL) services.

Logistics

Second-largest container carrier worldwide. Direct service from Lagos to Mediterranean and Northern European ports. Competitive rates for 20ft and 40ft containers.

ABC Transport

Partnership Strategy

NaijaSwap's buyer acquisition uses a multi-channel partnership approach:

  • Direct outreach — qualified European importers and distributors
  • Trade databases — verified buyer lists with A/B/C priority classification
  • NEPC — official Nigerian export promotion and matchmaking
  • EUNAP — EU-Nigeria agribusiness platform access
  • Enterprise Europe Network — European companies actively seeking African partners
  • Shipping lines — Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Grimaldi for logistics partnerships
  • Inspection bodies — SGS, Bureau Veritas, Intertek for quality assurance
  • Trade fairs — physical presence at Gulfood, SIAL Paris, Anuga Cologne
  • LinkedIn — professional network outreach to procurement managers
  • Referrals — leveraging existing B/L track record and relationships
  • Historical buyers — reactivating previous European customers
