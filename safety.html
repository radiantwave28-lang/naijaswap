🛡️ Safety Tips

Your safety is our top priority. Follow these guidelines to stay protected while buying and selling on NijaSwap.

🔒

Escrow Protection Active

All transactions on NijaSwap are protected by our secure escrow system. Never send money outside the platform.

🛒 For Buyers

✅

Verify the Seller

Always check the seller's profile, ratings, and verification status before making a purchase. Look for the "Verified Seller" badge.

💳

Use Escrow Payments

Never pay outside NijaSwap. Our escrow holds your payment until you confirm receipt of the item. This is your strongest protection.

📸

Request More Photos

Ask the seller for additional photos or a video of the item. Scammers often use stock images or photos from other listings.

💬

Chat Before Buying

Use our in-app chat to communicate with the seller. Keep all conversations on the platform for your protection.

🚚

Track Your Delivery

Always use tracked shipping. Request a tracking number and monitor your delivery on the Delivery page.

⭐

Leave Reviews

After receiving your item, leave an honest review. This helps other buyers make informed decisions and holds sellers accountable.

📦 For Sellers

📋

Provide Accurate Descriptions

Be honest about the condition of your item. Include clear photos from multiple angles and mention any defects or wear.

📦

Package Items Securely

Use appropriate packaging to prevent damage during shipping. Consider insurance for high-value items.

📤

Ship Promptly

Ship items within 48 hours of receiving payment. Update the tracking information immediately on the platform.

🤝

Be Responsive

Reply to buyer messages within 24 hours. Good communication builds trust and leads to positive reviews.

🏦

Verify Your Bank Details

Ensure your bank account details in your Wallet are correct to avoid payment delays.

⚠️

Report Suspicious Buyers

If a buyer asks you to communicate outside the app or requests unusual payment methods, report them immediately.

🚩 Red Flags — Report Immediately

  • Off-platform payments: Anyone asking you to pay via WhatsApp, direct bank transfer, or cash before delivery.
  • Too-good-to-be-true prices: An iPhone 15 Pro for ₦50,000 is a scam. Always verify market prices.
  • Urgency pressure: Sellers or buyers creating false urgency ("Only 1 left! Pay now or lose it!").
  • Requests for personal info: Never share your BVN, PIN, OTP, or password with anyone on the platform.
  • Shipping to unusual addresses: Be cautious of buyers requesting delivery to P.O. boxes or third-party addresses without verification.
  • No profile photo or history: New accounts with no reviews, no listings, and no verification should be treated with caution.
  • Overpayment scams: A buyer "accidentally" overpays and asks for a refund of the difference. This is a classic scam.

🔐 General Security

🔑

Use a Strong Password

Use a unique password with at least 12 characters including letters, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse passwords from other sites.

📱

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of security to your account. Go to your Profile settings to enable 2FA.

🌐

Check the URL

Always ensure you are on naijaswap-new.firebaseapp.com or our official domain. Beware of phishing sites.

🔄

Keep Your App Updated

Always use the latest version of NijaSwap to benefit from the latest security patches and features.

⚠️ Encountered a Problem?

If you suspect fraud, harassment, or any suspicious activity, report it immediately. Our team investigates every report within 24 hours.
